About Me

I am a Research Fellow at the Korea Institute for Advanced Study. My research interests include moduli spaces of sheaves and Higgs bundles, enumerative geometry, perverse sheaves, and representation theory.

I received my Ph.D. in 2026 under the supervision of Mark Andrea de Cataldo.
